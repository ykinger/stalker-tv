# Changelog

VibeTV uses `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` for stable releases and a prerelease suffix
(`-alpha.N`, `-beta.N`, or `-rc.N`) before a stable release.

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 (versionCode 2)

- Connect and save Stalker Portal playlists and browse live TV, movies, and shows.
- Search channels through a persisted local index and page through portal VOD results.
- Play channels and on-demand titles with Media3 and TV remote controls.
- Combine playlist-preparation progress into a single indicator.

This is a prerelease. Portal compatibility and playback behavior are still being
verified; no Play Store release is implied.
